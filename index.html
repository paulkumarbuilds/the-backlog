How This Works

The Backlog is a running archive of notes, PYQs, and reference material — organized so you're not hunting across ten different WhatsApp groups and Drive links to find what you need for an exam. Everything here traces back to what seniors and batchmates actually shared, kept in one place and updated as more comes in.

  • Every subject card shows credits, a quick look at what's covered, and the current teacher. Click See more for the full year-by-year history.
  • The search bar looks across every semester at once, no matter which tab you're on.
  • The Books button opens the reference textbooks for that subject — same books regardless of who's teaching.
  • A red dashed VERIFIED stamp means the material's been checked and confirmed working.
  • A solid red BROKEN / MISSING stamp means something on that subject's page needs attention — a dead link, a missing file, or content that hasn't been checked yet.
  • PYQs and Reference Notes are always listed if they exist. Student Notes and Assignments live under Additional Material, since they don't exist for every subject yet.
  • On a subject page, Download All PYQs bundles every year's paper into one zip.
